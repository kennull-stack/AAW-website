AAW CIEM care system

How to Service Your IEM

Custom in-ear monitors live close to skin, sweat, dust, and daily handling. A short care routine keeps the shells comfortable, the sound bores open, and the cable connection dependable.

After every session

  1. Brush or wipe loose debris from the shell.
  2. Check the sound bores before wax hardens.
  3. Let moisture evaporate before closing the case.
  4. Store the monitors and cable without tight bends.

The routine

Six small habits that protect fit and sound

Work gently, always clean outward, and avoid forcing tools into openings. If debris is packed deep inside a sound bore, stop and contact AAW support rather than pushing further.

Step 01

Brush loose dust away

Use a soft brush to clear lint, dry skin, and surface dust from the shell, faceplate edge, and nozzle exterior. Keep the motion light so particles lift away instead of being pressed into seams.

  • Brush outward from openings and edges.
  • Avoid alcohol, solvents, and rough fabric on polished shells.
  • Do this before deeper bore cleaning.
Step 02

Clear the sound bores

Use the included cleaning tool to gently remove earwax or dust from the sound bores. Be careful not to push debris deeper.

  • Hold the bore downward when possible.
  • Scoop from the opening; do not dig into the tube.
  • Stop if the wax is hardened or lodged deep inside.
Step 03

Care for cable contacts

Disconnect by holding the connector housing, then inspect the pins and socket area for lint or residue. Clean dry debris with a soft brush before reconnecting.

  • Never pull the cable itself to disconnect.
  • Reconnect straight, without twisting the pins.
  • Do not use liquid cleaner on exposed contacts.
Step 04

Control moisture

After use, let the monitors air dry on a clean surface before sealing them in the case. Add a desiccant pack to help absorb moisture from sweat, humidity, or stage use.

  • Replace desiccant packs when saturated or expired.
  • Keep monitors away from heat, dryers, and direct sun.
  • If exposed to water, disconnect the cable and let everything dry fully.
Step 05

Store without stress

Return the monitors to their protective case once they are clean and dry. Coil the cable loosely so the connectors and memory wire are not under tension.

  • Use the case whenever the monitors are not in your ears.
  • Keep loose tools, keys, and metal objects away from the shells.
  • Do not store monitors in pockets or bags without protection.
Step 06

Send blocked mesh or cracked shells for service

For universal monitors with mesh-equipped nozzles, bring the monitor to AAW or an authorized dealer if earwax clogs the mesh. If UIEM or CIEM shells are cracked, bring them to AAW for repair or reshell assessment.

  • Do not pick, pierce, or push through a clogged mesh.
  • Use AAW or an authorized dealer for mesh replacement.
  • Stop using cracked shells until they are assessed for repair or reshell.

Service rhythm

When to clean, dry, and ask for help

A little care after each session prevents most problems. Professional service is for blocked bores, loose parts, visible cracks, intermittent channels, or anything that feels forced.

Every use

Wipe or brush the shells, check the bore openings, and let moisture dry before storage.

Weekly

Inspect cable contacts, case padding, desiccant condition, and the shell surface under good light.

Send for service

Contact AAW if the bore is blocked deep inside, the fit changes suddenly, the shell is damaged, or one side becomes intermittent.

Need a deeper service?

For blocked sound tubes, damaged shells, cable faults, or refit questions, get in touch with AAW before attempting aggressive cleaning at home.
