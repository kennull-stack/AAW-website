Design Story

Hybrid weight without losing coherence.

ASH is built from AAW's long-running hybrid idea: use a dynamic driver for the physical foundation that balanced armatures rarely reproduce naturally, then let dedicated armatures take over upper midrange, treble, and super-high extension.

The 13mm graphene diaphragm is tuned diaphragm-first, then constrained with physical low-pass filtering so it avoids unnecessary overlap with the armatures. Driver position, acoustic bore layout, and TrueXross crossover behavior are treated together so the arrival timing stays organized.

The result is a big, textured, bass-capable monitor that still keeps vocal edges, cymbal air, and realistic stage cues intact.

Acoustic Architecture

Graphene dynamic bass. Six-armature precision. TrueXross control.

ASH lets the 13mm graphene dynamic driver carry bass and lower midrange, then hands upper bands to six balanced armatures. The 4-way TrueXross system avoids the chaotic overlap that can make hybrid monitors sound powerful but poorly timed.

FAQ

Before you choose ASH

What is the ASH driver configuration?

ASH uses one 13mm graphene diaphragm dynamic driver and six balanced armature drivers in a 4-way TrueXross hybrid system.

Why use a 13mm graphene dynamic driver?

The large graphene driver gives ASH its physical bass and lower-mid body. Its size, stiffness, and low mass help produce a powerful yet natural foundation.

Is ASH suitable for stage use?

Yes. ASH was designed for band performances, artists, worship events, and listeners who want a large, immersive hybrid signature. Final stage suitability depends on fit, isolation needs, and monitoring volume.

Should I choose universal or custom fit?

Choose universal fit for quicker auditioning and easier sharing. Choose custom fit for ear-impression shells, stronger passive isolation, and a more personal long-session fit.

Built by Advanced AcousticWerkes

Singapore hybrid IEM craft, since 2014.

Those who listen will find the way.

Advanced AcousticWerkes (AAW) designs and builds in-ear monitors in Singapore. Founded by Kevin Wang, AAW works across dynamic, balanced armature, electrostatic, planar magnetic, and hybrid platforms. Every model is shaped by physical prototyping and ear-impression feedback, not preset curves.

ASH distills the hybrid approach into a seven-driver platform: a 13mm graphene dynamic woofer for bass weight, six balanced armatures for mid and treble precision, and TrueXross crossover timing that keeps all seven drivers coherent. It is built for listeners who want physical low-end authority without sacrificing vocal clarity or stage depth.
