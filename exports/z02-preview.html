Design Story

A dynamic-planar hybrid with AAW digital acoustic design.

The Z02 showcases an innovative dynamic-planar hybrid multi-driver system, meticulously tuned for a modern and lively sound signature. It delivers a natural and robust sub-bass response, unmatched by traditional all-balanced armature setups, while its miniature planar magnetic tweeter keeps vocal and treble detail clean, extended, and smooth.

The planar magnetic tweeter is embedded deep into the Z02's 3D-printed shell. Its dedicated cavity design eliminates sound pressure decay and unwanted resonance common to traditional tubed balanced armature designs. The thin diaphragm responds quickly to signal changes, giving Z02 accurate dynamics, low distortion, and a consistent soundstage.

With a wholly digitized design process, Z02 employs AAW third-generation digital enclosure and acoustic chamber design. Driver and nozzle placements are simulated through hundreds of iterations to determine ideal phasing and response, then 3D printed to precision for production consistency.

Suitable for rising artists, band performances, streaming platforms, worship events, audio engineering, and anybody who appreciates a critical listening experience.

Fitment Options

Designed for a stable universal seal.

Z02 ships with assorted ear tips so listeners can tune seal, comfort, bass weight, and treble energy by fit. The compact shell is shaped through AAW's digital enclosure process for ergonomic daily use.

Acoustic Architecture

Planar speed. Graphene low end. One coherent physical crossover.

The miniature planar magnetic tweeter handles upper detail with low distortion, wide dispersion, and fast transient response. The 10mm graphene dynamic driver uses a rigid ultra-thin diaphragm and physical low-pass filtering to keep bass focused with minimal overlap. Driver placement and acoustic chambers are digitally simulated, then 3D printed for consistency.

FAQ

Before you choose Z02

What is the Z02 driver configuration?

Z02 uses a 2-driver dynamic-planar hybrid configuration: a 10mm graphene dynamic driver for low frequencies and a miniature planar magnetic tweeter for vocal, treble, and upper-frequency detail.

What does the miniature planar magnetic tweeter do?

The planar magnetic tweeter is embedded in the 3D-printed shell to reduce sound pressure decay and unwanted resonance typical of tubed balanced armature layouts. It is designed for extended high-frequency response, low distortion, fast transient response, and consistent soundstage reproduction.

What does the graphene dynamic driver add?

The 10mm graphene dynamic driver uses an ultra-thin rigid diaphragm and physical low-pass filtering so it can handle bass and lower-frequency energy with minimal overlap against the planar tweeter.

How is the Z02 enclosure made?

Z02 uses AAW's third-generation digital enclosure and acoustic chamber process. Driver and nozzle placements are simulated through many iterations, then 3D printed for precision and production consistency.

What comes in the box?

48 inch Brevity OCC copper detachable cable, AAW carrying case, assorted ear tips, and a 1-year limited warranty.

Singapore IEM craft, shaped by listening.

Advanced AcousticWerkes (AAW) is a Singapore-based IEM manufacturer founded in 2014 by Kevin Wang. AAW designs and builds custom and universal in-ear monitors across dynamic, balanced armature, electrostatic, planar, and hybrid platforms.

Z02 brings that approach into a compact dynamic-planar hybrid platform: miniature planar tweeter, 10mm graphene dynamic driver, physical crossover, OCC copper internal wire, and AAW's third-generation 3D-printed acoustic chamber design.
