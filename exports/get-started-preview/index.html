How It Works

Five steps from order to first listen

The path to a custom monitor is simpler than it sounds. Most of the work happens in our workshop, and we guide you through the parts that need your attention.

01

Place Your Order

Choose your model on our website. Every AAW custom IEM starts with one of our acoustic platforms: from the entry-level A3H+ Lux to the flagship Canary Pro. Add your faceplate design, cable choice, and any personal engraving during checkout.

02

Get Your Ear Impressions Taken

Visit a certified audiologist for silicone ear impressions. The impression needs to capture the full shape of your ear canal and concha, including the second bend. We provide an imprinting guide you can bring to your appointment. At our Singapore showroom, we offer the service at SGD $50.

03

Send the Impressions to Us

Ship your impressions with your order confirmation and adequate protection to our Singapore workshop. If your audiologist offers 3D scanning, you can also send the digital STL or HPS files to support@aaw.me. Either way, we confirm receipt within one business day.

04

Manufacturing Begins

Once your impressions arrive, our lab team 3D-scans them, models your shell geometry, and begins the build. Drivers are installed and crossed over by hand. The faceplate is finished to your specification. Each pair is measured and listened to before it leaves the bench.

05

We Ship Your CIEMs to You

Your completed monitors ship with a carry case, cleaning tool, cable, and documentation. Tracking is provided the moment the package leaves Singapore. Most international deliveries arrive within three to seven business days.

Ear Impressions

The foundation of a perfect fit

An ear impression is a silicone cast of your ear canal and outer ear. It captures the unique shape that no universal tip can fully match. When done well, the resulting monitor sits flush, seals without pressure, and stays put through long sessions.

We strongly recommend visiting a professionally certified audiologist for this step. A good impression goes past the second bend of the ear canal and includes the full concha and helix area. An incomplete impression leads to a loose fit, and a remake takes time.

Showroom service available

If you are in Singapore, we offer ear impression taking at our showroom for SGD $50. The process takes about 10–20 minutes. Please have your ears cleared before coming for the best result.

If you need help locating an audiologist near you, email us at support@aaw.me and we will do our best to point you in the right direction.
